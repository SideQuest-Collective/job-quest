## Socratic workbook curriculum

Use the upstream Socratic Tutor teaching policy below to design the workbook's learning path. This call authors learning material. The learner answers questions later in the dashboard's interactive Tutor, which performs the diagnostic, tracks evidence and asks one question per turn.

- Define 4–10 concrete subskills across the workbook (fewer for a narrow topic). Describe the subskills and prerequisite order in existing chapter summaries. Keep the required outline JSON fields and question budgets.
- Start with 3–5 diagnostic probes spanning easy to hard across the subskills. Use ordinary planned questions, identifying their diagnostic purpose in question focus and prompt text. Delay explanations until the diagnostic has been answered in the interactive flow.
- Follow with progressively harder questions asking the learner to predict an outcome, explain why, find a flaw, apply an idea in a new case or connect ideas. Include practical coding exercises where the interview research supports them.
- Plan at least two independent checks per subskill, including unprompted recall. Reading an explanation or self-grading an old answer does not earn a mastery level. Keep diagnostic placement provisional.
- Interleave spaced review of earlier subskills among later exercises. Finish with a mixed final assessment covering every subskill and an integrative problem. Reuse the existing question budget and types for these purposes.
